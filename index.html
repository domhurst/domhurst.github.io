Dominic Hurst

I build teams.

I’m currently Chief of Staff at Monumental. We’re on a mission to make housing beautiful and affordable with robotics & software.

About

I’m from a family of builders and grew up, near London, around construction sites.

Then I moved into startups, leading recruitment at Polystream (acquired by Mythical Games) and Sphere (acquired by Twitter). I was in the founding operations team at OakNorth AI, and then joined Meta | Meta AI Research.

I started my own company, and then found Monumental - and knew this was what I wanted to do. I always dreamed of building houses, now I’m building the team that is doing it for the first time ever with robots.

With Atrium, our OS for onsite construction, and our fleet of robots, we have built over one hundred and ten homes, hundreds of metres of canal walls, a school, a sports stadium and a hotel.

I’m a generalist that likes to work at the intersection of people, operations and execution. I’ve helped launch new markets, shaped roles that don’t exist, and found the best people for them - often in the most unlikely places.
